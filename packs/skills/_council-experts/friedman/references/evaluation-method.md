# friedman — finding shape and usability-test protocol

Platform: all

## When this applies

Any UX review or plan that ranks problems, or proposes watching users. Read it before writing findings.

## Addendum (2026-10-28) — five fields per finding

Source: nielsen-heuristics, nngroup-severity

- Each finding names one of Nielsen's ten heuristics, a 0–4 severity, the evidence (screen, state, element, what the user sees or cannot do), the smallest checkable fix, and the platform (web, telegram-mini-app, ios, android).
- Severity weighs frequency, impact and persistence together: 4 catastrophe, 3 major, 2 minor, 1 cosmetic, 0 not a problem. Map to Council levels as 4 → P1, 3 → P2, 1–2 → P3; drop 0.
- Several reviewers rate independently before merging; one reviewer finds only part of the problems.

## Addendum (2026-10-28) — when a plan proposes a usability test

Source: nngroup-usability-testing, govuk-service-manual-research

Five participants from the real audience per round, three to five goal-phrased tasks without interface words, think-aloud with a facilitator who does not lead, per-task success / partial / fail plus time on task, findings in the five-field shape, and a re-test after the fixes.
